HP / Agilent / Keysight 34401A 6½-digit multimeter.

**The reading channel is named after the `mode`**: `Vdc`, `Vac`, `Idc`, `Iac`,
`R2w` or `R4w`. An alias on `dmm.Vdc` therefore cannot record a current by mistake
after somebody switched the meter to `Idc`. It reports an error until the mode is
back.

* **Settings**: `range` (or auto), `nplc`, `autozero` (DC and resistance), and
  `ac_filter` (AC).
* **Timeouts**: reading timeouts follow the NPLC and the AC filter.
* **Overload**: `9.9E37` reads as ±inf.

It is reachable over GPIB and RS-232. Several 34401As can share a Prologix adapter:
use the options `shared` and `gpib_addr`.
