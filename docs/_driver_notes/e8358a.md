Agilent (now Keysight) PNA series vector network analyzers: the E8358A (300 kHz –
9 GHz), and the E8356A (3 GHz) and E8357A (6 GHz), which use the same commands. Connect
over LAN (`TCPIP0::<ip>::inst0::INSTR`, or the socket `TCPIP0::<ip>::5025::SOCKET`) or
GPIB.

* **Traces**: `S11`, `S21`, `S12`, `S22` as log magnitude (dB), with `…_phase` (degrees)
  and `…_re` / `…_im` (the complex value). Each is a whole sweep; its x axis is
  `frequencies` (Hz), which the plots and the data files use.
* **Source**: `power` (dBm) is an ordinary channel: alias it with limits and sweep it,
  for a power dependence.
* **Settings**: `f_start`, `f_stop`, `f_center`, `f_span`, `points` (1 – 16001),
  `if_bandwidth` (the analyzer's list, 1 Hz – 40 kHz), `sweep_type` (LIN / LOG),
  `averaging` and `average_count`, `output` (RF on / off) and `correction` (the
  channel's calibration, made on the analyzer).
* **Actions**: `single_sweep`, `continuous` (free run, for the front panel),
  `delete_traces` and `preset` (below).

**Measurements.** The driver creates a measurement on channel 1 of the analyzer
(`EM_S21`, …) the first time an S-parameter is read, and shows it in window 1. The
analyzer's other measurements are left alone. With the option `parameters`, e.g.
`"S21,S11"`, they are created when connecting, so that the first sweep already
measures them all. Option `channel` uses another channel of the analyzer.

**One sweep per point.** Reading a trace starts a single sweep of all measurements and
waits for it to end. The other traces of that sweep (`S21_phase`, `S11` …) are read from
the same sweep: recording `S21` and `S21_phase` at a point costs one sweep. With
averaging on, a reading is always a complete, fresh average of `average_count` sweeps.
The sweep time is about points / IF bandwidth per direction; S12 or S22 together with
S11 or S21 adds the reverse sweep. Afterwards the analyzer holds the last sweep on its
screen; `continuous` lets it sweep freely again.

**Starting clean: `delete_traces`.** The analyzer sweeps every measurement on the
channel, also ones made at its front panel and ones read earlier: a leftover S12 or
S22 adds the reverse sweep and roughly doubles the time of every sweep. `delete_traces`
deletes every measurement on the analyzer and leaves every setting as it is; afterwards
only what is read is measured. Make it the first pre-sweep constant of a sweep (channel
`vna.delete_traces`, no value):

```json
"pre": {"constants": [{"channel": "vna.delete_traces"},
                      {"channel": "vna.if_bandwidth", "value": 1000}]}
```

**`preset`** is the analyzer's factory preset (`SYSTem:FPReset`): every setting goes back
to its factory default - a wide span, 201 points, 35 kHz IF bandwidth, **0 dBm with
RF on**, no averaging, correction off (check the values on your analyzer: options and
firmware change them) - and every measurement and window is deleted. Calibrations saved on the analyzer stay, but are no longer applied. Mind the
power: a device that wants −60 dBm gets 0 dBm. To start clean but keep the settings,
use `delete_traces`.

A sweep that has not ended after three times the expected time plus `sweep_timeout`
(30 s) is an error. A setting the analyzer refuses (out of range, `correction` without
a calibration …) raises with the analyzer's own message.

`SIM::e8358a` is an emulator: a hanger resonator at 5 GHz behind a lossy cable, with
noise that falls with power, IF bandwidth and averaging.
