Stanford Research Systems SR830 and SR850 (and SR810) DSP lock-in amplifiers.

* **Readings**: `X`, `Y`, `R`, `theta`, the noise readings `Xn` / `Yn`, and the
  auxiliary inputs `aux_in1…4`.
* **Sources**: `sine_out` (amplitude), `frequency`, `phase` and `aux_out1…4`. These are
  ordinary channels: alias them with limits and ramp rates, and sweep them.
* **Settings**: `reference` (internal / external), `sensitivity`, `time_constant`,
  `rolloff`, `coupling`, `ground`, `line_filter`, `input`, `reserve`, `harmonic` and
  `sync_filter`. Sensitivities and time constants are human-readable (`"500 µV"`,
  `"300 ms"`).
* **Actions**: auto gain, auto phase, auto reserve.

With a current input (`input` = I), the sensitivities are listed in A, and X / Y / R
are in A. `frequency` is read-only with an external reference.

`Xn` / `Yn` are measured on the instrument's displays. On the SR830, CH1 / CH2 are
set to X / Y noise on the first read; on the SR850, traces 3 and 4 are used.

Set `pre_delay` in sweeps to a few time constants.
