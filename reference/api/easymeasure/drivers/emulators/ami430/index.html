    easymeasure.drivers.emulators.ami430¶

    Emulated American Magnetics Model 430 programmer with a superconducting magnet.

    Commands and queries are those of the Model 430 manual (remote interface reference): STATE?, FIELD:MAGnet?, CONFigure:FIELD:TARGet, RAMP, PAUSE, ZERO, PSwitch, QUench ... and the IEEE-488.2 *IDN?, *CLS, SYSTem:ERRor?.

    The magnet behaves like one, in (scaled) real time:

    • the supply ramps its current at the segment-1 rate while the magnet is driven (switch heater on), at the faster "PSw P/S ramp rate" while the switch is cold (then only the current in the leads changes, the magnet keeps its persistent current);
    • PSwitch 1 heats the switch for the heated time (STATE 9), PSwitch 0 cools it for the cooled time (STATE 10); ramping is refused meanwhile;
    • when the switch turns resistive the supply current is forced into the magnet: a difference of more than mismatch_quench A quenches the magnet, as heating a switch at the wrong current can do;
    • a quench (quench() from a test, or a mismatch) sets STATE 7 and QUench? 1, zeroes the supply and lets the magnet current decay; QUench 0 clears it (the supply then PAUSEs).

    Options: time_scale (simulated seconds per real second), coil (T/A), limit (A), rate (segment-1 rate, A/s), psw_rate (A/s), heat_time, cool_time (s), switch (a persistent switch is installed), units (0 = kG, 1 = T), rate_units (0 = per second, 1 = per minute), inductance (H), mismatch_quench (A), auto_zero (ramp the leads to zero after cooling, as the front panel does), greeting (the TCP welcome lines), model (430 or 420).
